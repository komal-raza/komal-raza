## 🔭 About Me

I'm a **Full-Stack Engineer with 4 years of experience** building web applications, Shopify apps, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, and business software.

My core expertise lies in **React, Node.js, Express.js, and PostgreSQL**, with hands-on experience developing merchant-facing Shopify applications, subscription and payment flows, analytics dashboards, background jobs, and third-party API integrations.

I enjoy working across the stack—from building responsive user interfaces and designing API integrations to implementing business logic, working with relational databases, and deploying applications.

Currently, I'm focused on building scalable digital products, improving my system design skills, and exploring modern AI-assisted software development workflows.

- 💼 **Current Role:** Senior Full-Stack Engineer at LeoaTech
- 🛍️ **Specialization:** Public and custom Shopify applications
- 🚀 **Product Focus:** Multi-tenant SaaS, subscription systems, and LMS platforms
- 🎨 **Frontend:** React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Shopify Polaris
- ⚙️ **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, and background jobs
- 🌍 **Work Preference:** Remote opportunities and international product teams

Most of my professional work is maintained in private repositories due to client and company policies. My portfolio showcases selected projects and case studies.

**[Explore My Portfolio →](https://v0-komal-raaza.vercel.app/)**

## 🚀 What I Build

- **Shopify Applications** — Embedded merchant dashboards, billing flows, referral campaigns, and media management tools.
- **Multi-Tenant SaaS Platforms** — Customer-specific workspaces, subscription plans, payment flows, and background processing.
- **Learning Management Systems** — Administrative portals, course management, attendance, homework, and reporting workflows.
- **Backend Services & APIs** — REST APIs, database-driven business logic, webhooks, and third-party integrations.
- **Payment Integrations** — Shopify Billing API, Stripe subscriptions, and Stripe Connect.
